# Tafsir Editions Reference

## Complete List of Supported Tafsirs

### 1. Tafsir Ibn Kathir (abridged)
- **Slug:** `en-tafisr-ibn-kathir`
- **Author:** Hafiz Ibn Kathir (1301-1373 CE)
- **Full Name:** Ismail ibn Kathir al-Dimashqi
- **Language:** English (translated from Arabic)
- **Methodology (Menhec):** `athari`, `riwayah`, `hadith-based`, `canonical`
- **Description:** One of the most famous and widely accepted tafsirs in Sunni Islam. Relies heavily on hadith, reports from the Prophet's companions, and early Islamic sources. Follows the Athari (textualist) school of thought.
- **Coverage:** Complete Quran
- **Source:** https://quran.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "In the Name of God the Compassionate the Merciful. This is the beginning of the Quran, and it is recommended to begin all matters with it."

### 2. Maarif-ul-Quran
- **Slug:** `en-tafsir-maarif-ul-quran`
- **Author:** Mufti Muhammad Shafi (1897-1976 CE)
- **Full Name:** Muhammad Shafi Deobandi
- **Language:** English (translated from Urdu)
- **Methodology:** `dirayah`, `jurisprudential`, `socio-moral`, `modernist-traditional`
- **Description:** Comprehensive tafsir with emphasis on jurisprudential rulings, moral lessons, and contemporary relevance. Written by the founder of Darul Uloom Karachi.
- **Coverage:** Complete Quran
- **Source:** https://quran.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "Bismillah - Beginning with the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. This verse teaches us to begin every good deed with Allah's name."

### 3. Tazkirul Quran
- **Slug:** `en-tazkirul-quran`
- **Author:** Maulana Wahid Uddin Khan (1925-2021 CE)
- **Language:** English
- **Methodology:** `dirayah`, `reflective`, `peace-oriented`, `da'wah-focused`
- **Description:** Modern tafsir focusing on spiritual reflection, peace, and contemporary application. Emphasizes the Quran's message for modern times and interfaith understanding.
- **Coverage:** Complete Quran
- **Source:** https://quran.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. This opening reminds us of God's infinite mercy and grace."

### 4. Kashf Al-Asrar Tafsir
- **Slug:** `en-kashf-al-asrar-tafsir`
- **Author:** Unknown (attributed to Maybudi, 11th century)
- **Language:** English (translated from Persian)
- **Methodology:** `ishari`, `sufi-mystical`, `linguistic`, `spiritual-exegesis`
- **Description:** One of the earliest and most important Persian Sufi tafsirs. Reveals inner meanings and spiritual secrets of the Quran. Combines linguistic analysis with mystical insights.
- **Coverage:** Partial (selected verses)
- **Source:** https://www.altafsir.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "Bismillah - The Name is the Named. When you say 'In the name of Allah,' you are invoking His very Essence."

### 5. Al Qushairi Tafsir
- **Slug:** `en-al-qushairi-tafsir`
- **Author:** Al-Qushayri (986-1072 CE)
- **Full Name:** Abd al-Karīm ibn Hawāzin al-Qushayri
- **Language:** English (translated from Arabic)
- **Methodology:** `ishari`, `tasawwuf`, `spiritual-allusions`, `kalam`
- **Description:** Sufi tafsir by one of the most important early Sufi scholars. Combines mystical interpretation with Ash'ari theology (kalam).
- **Coverage:** Partial (selected verses)
- **Source:** https://www.altafsir.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "The Basmala contains all divine names. 'Allah' is the Essence, 'Rahman' is universal mercy, 'Rahim' is specific mercy for believers."

### 6. Kashani Tafsir
- **Slug:** `en-kashani-tafsir`
- **Author:** Abd al-Razzaq al-Kashani (died 1329 CE)
- **Language:** English (translated from Arabic)
- **Methodology:** `ishari`, `tasawwuf`, `philosophical-mysticism`, `irfan`
- **Description:** Philosophical-mystical tafsir influenced by Ibn Arabi's school of thought. Focuses on unveiling (kashf) of spiritual realities.
- **Coverage:** Partial (selected verses)
- **Source:** https://www.altafsir.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "The Name manifests the Named. Bismillah is the point where the Divine Essence meets creation."

### 7. Tafsir al-Tustari
- **Slug:** `en-tafsir-al-tustari`
- **Author:** Sahl al-Tustari (818-896 CE)
- **Full Name:** Sahl ibn Abdullah al-Tustari
- **Language:** English (translated from Arabic)
- **Methodology:** `ishari`, `early-sufi`, `spiritual-significance`
- **Description:** One of the earliest surviving Sufi tafsirs. Brief, profound statements revealing spiritual significance of verses.
- **Coverage:** Partial (selected verses)
- **Source:** https://www.altafsir.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "Bismillah - Remembering Allah is the key to all doors."

### 8. Asbab Al-Nuzul by Al-Wahidi
- **Slug:** `en-asbab-al-nuzul-by-al-wahidi`
- **Author:** Al-Wahidi (died 1076 CE)
- **Full Name:** Ali ibn Ahmad al-Wahidi al-Naysaburi
- **Language:** English (translated from Arabic)
- **Methodology:** `riwayah`, `asbab-al-nuzul`, `occasions-of-revelation`, `historical-context`
- **Description:** Classical work on the occasions of revelation (asbab al-nuzul). Explains historical context and circumstances surrounding Quranic revelations.
- **Coverage:** Selected verses with known occasions
- **Source:** https://www.altafsir.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "The Basmala was revealed to teach Muslims to begin all matters with Allah's name."

### 9. Tafsir Ibn Abbas
- **Slug:** `en-tafsir-ibn-abbas`
- **Attributed to:** Abdullah ibn Abbas (619-687 CE)
- **Full Name:** Abdullah ibn Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib
- **Language:** English (translated from Arabic)
- **Methodology:** `riwayah`, `narrative-based`, `early-tradition`
- **Description:** Attributed to the Prophet's cousin, known as "the interpreter of the Quran." Contains early interpretations and narrations from the time of the Prophet.
- **Coverage:** Complete Quran
- **Source:** https://www.altafsir.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "Bismillah means: I begin with every name that belongs to Allah, for 'name' means 'named,' and Allah is all His beautiful names."

### 10. Al-Jalalayn
- **Slug:** `en-al-jalalayn`
- **Authors:** Jalal al-Din al-Mahalli (1389-1459 CE) and Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti (1445-1505 CE)
- **Language:** English (translated from Arabic)
- **Methodology:** `dirayah`, `concise-literal`, `linguistic`, `classical-manual`
- **Description:** Concise, widely studied tafsir known for its brevity and clarity. Focuses on grammatical analysis and literal meaning. Used as a standard textbook in Islamic schools.
- **Coverage:** Complete Quran
- **Source:** https://www.altafsir.com/
- **Sample Text (Surah 1:1):** "In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful: This is a prepositional phrase that is semantically connected to an implied verb, such as 'I begin.'"

## Methodology Comparison

### Riwayah (Narrative-Based) Tafsirs
- **Primary Sources:** Hadith, companion reports, early Islamic narratives
- **Approach:** Textual, historical, traditional
- **Best for:** Understanding historical context, Prophetic explanations
- **Representatives:** Ibn Kathir, Ibn Abbas, Asbab al-Nuzul

### Dirayah (Rational/Jurisprudential) Tafsirs
- **Primary Sources:** Reason, linguistic analysis, legal principles
- **Approach:** Analytical, legal, contemporary application
- **Best for:** Legal rulings, moral lessons, modern relevance
- **Representatives:** Maarif-ul-Quran, Al-Jalalayn, Tazkirul Quran

### Ishari (Mystical/Allegorical) Tafsirs
- **Primary Sources:** Spiritual insight, inner meaning, mystical experience
- **Approach:** Symbolic, allegorical, spiritual
- **Best for:** Spiritual growth, inner dimensions, Sufi perspectives
- **Representatives:** Kashf al-Asrar, Al-Qushairi, Kashani, Tustari

- Kashf al-Asrar Tafsir
- Al Qushairi Tafsir

- Tafsir al-Tustari

### Availability by Surah
- **Early Meccan Surahs (1-5):** Well covered by all tafsirs
- **Long Medinan Surahs (2, 3, 4, 5):** Complete in major tafsirs, partial in mystical ones
- **Short Meccan Surahs (81-114):** Well covered, especially in mystical tafsirs

## Historical Context

### Timeline of Tafsir Development
1. **Early Period (7th-8th century):** Oral explanations by Prophet and companions
2. **Formative Period (8th-9th century):** Written collections begin (Ibn Abbas attribution)
3. **Classical Period (9th-12th century):** Systematic tafsirs emerge (Tabari, Tustari)
4. **Medieval Period (12th-15th century):** Specialization (Ibn Kathir - hadith, Qushairi - Sufi)
5. **Modern Period (19th-21st century):** Contemporary approaches (Maarif, Tazkirul)

### School of Thought Affiliation
- **Sunni Traditional:** Ibn Kathir, Al-Jalalayn
- **Deobandi:** Maarif-ul-Quran
- **Modernist:** Tazkirul Quran
- **Sufi:** Kashf al-Asrar, Al-Qushairi, Kashani, Tustari
- **Early Tradition:** Ibn Abbas, Asbab al-Nuzul

## Usage Recommendations

### For Beginners
1. **Start with:** Al-Jalalayn (concise, grammatical)
2. **Then try:** Ibn Kathir (detailed, traditional)
3. **For context:** Asbab al-Nuzul (historical occasions)

### For Spiritual Seekers
1. **Start with:** Tustari (brief, profound)
2. **Then try:** Kashf al-Asrar (detailed mystical)
3. **For theology:** Al-Qushairi (Sufi + kalam)

### For Legal/Contemporary Issues
1. **Start with:** Maarif-ul-Quran (jurisprudential)
2. **For modern application:** Tazkirul Quran
3. **For detailed fiqh:** Consult specialized works

### For Academic Study
1. **Textual analysis:** Al-Jalalayn (linguistic)
2. **Historical context:** Ibn Kathir + Asbab al-Nuzul
3. **Comparative study:** Multiple tafsirs for same verse
